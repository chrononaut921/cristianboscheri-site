Stream of Unconsciousness

2024 · 60 min · dance comedy

A few years ago Cristian had a brief moment of absolute clarity, and he has been trying to get it back ever since. In this solo he turns scientist and builds a method to control human perception, where dance, quirky humour and mathematics meet to harvest insights from the audience.

The Odd Ones

2024 · contemporary dance and circus · a show by Simon Granit Ossoinak

An acrobat, a contemporary dancer and a breakdancer, each with their own way of moving and communicating, look for a way to be together without giving up who they are. Cristian co-created and performed it with Simon Granit Ossoinak and Stasy Terehhova.

It premiered at Theater Rotterdam during Circusstad Festival in May 2024 and toured the Netherlands, Belgium, Finland, Sweden and the UK.

“Three completely different dancers develop a connection with each other through choreography and humour.”

Het Parool (translated)

I Am Deep

2023 · 10 min · breaking and stand-up

People often tell Cristian that his dancing looks profound. In this short piece he finally explains what it means, mixing breaking with stand-up and speaking to an audience, something he as a dancer is not entirely comfortable doing.
