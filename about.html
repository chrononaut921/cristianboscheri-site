About

Cristian Boscheri

Interdisciplinary artist, Rotterdam.

Cristian Boscheri is an Italian artist based in Rotterdam who works across theatre, dance, comedy and speculative futures. His work moves between breaking, clowning, mime and stand-up, and explores perception, paradox and comedy as a language, physical, verbal or both. With Timescaping, his most recent practice, he explores how imagining the future shapes the way we see the present.

He started breaking in Italy in 2005 and won the Italian national title in 2009. In 2013 the Open Your Mind battle first showed him other ways of being on stage, leading him to contemporary dance, physical theatre, improvisation and clowning. He came to the Netherlands in 2012 to study Sustainable Energy Technology at TU Eindhoven, where he later joined the post-master programme Smart Energy Buildings & Cities. From 2016 he co-directed the cultural organisation Stichting wedowe for eight years, producing short films with young makers, programming and hosting the BrainCandies live events and running hip hop projects in Nepal, India, Brazil, Bosnia and Morocco.

His solo shows include How to Enjoy Life (2023), Stream of Unconsciousness (2024), Pleaser (2025), co-produced by Circusstad, and Heavy Meta (2026), his first hour of comedy. His work has been presented at Theater Rotterdam during Circusstad, Festival Boulevard, Korzo, CircusBende and Amsterdam Fringe Festival. With Simon Granit and Stasy Terehhova he co-created The Odd Ones, which toured the Netherlands, Belgium, Sweden, Finland and the UK. On comedy stages he has performed more than a hundred sets in English, Italian and Dutch.

Theaterkrant called Pleaser “more than a dance solo, more than a clown act”. In 2023 he became world champion at the Open Your Mind experimental dance battle in Eindhoven, and in 2025 he was a finalist in both the Comedy Talent Award and the English Comedy Talent Award in Utrecht. His work has been supported by Fonds Podiumkunsten, Cultuurfonds, Amarte, Amsterdams Fonds voor de Kunst and Gemeente Rotterdam.

Since 2024 he has been developing Timescaping, a practice of imagining aspects of society a hundred years from now through live sessions, stories and visual explorations. He started it in response to feeling overwhelmed by a world that was changing faster than he could make sense of it. It has become an ongoing search for ways to build our capacity to stay with uncertainty, together: a space where different perspectives and disagreement can exist, and where people have permission to speculate without needing to be right, rooted in the belief that the way we imagine the future shapes how we act today. What drives him just as much is the pleasure of speculating itself: building, with nuance and a taste for the unexpected, the stories and worlds of things that don’t exist yet, but one day might.

So far he has written more than a hundred scenario cards and led over ten sessions, for groups of four to a hundred and thirty people. They range from public evenings he organised himself in Rotterdam and Utrecht to sessions for universities, cultural organisations and companies. A growing part of the practice lives outside the sessions, in writing and images, from a printed card deck to short stories and artefacts from the future. Since 2026 he has also been an associate artist with Arteconomy in Belgium, an organisation that brings artists into other sectors to spark new thinking.

He also teaches Applied Floor Acrobatics, a specialisation course at Codarts Circus Arts in Rotterdam, as well as dance-comedy workshops where participants write jokes with the body, and he hosts and moderates events.
