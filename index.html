About

Cristian Boscheri is an Italian artist based in Rotterdam. He worked as an energy engineer, co-directed the cultural organisation Stichting wedowe for eight years, and has danced breaking for twenty. Today he makes solo shows that mix theatre, clown, dance and comedy. Since 2024 he has been developing Timescaping, a practice of imagining aspects of society a hundred years from now through live sessions, stories and visual explorations.
