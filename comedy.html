Comedy

Stand-up, alternative comedy and storytelling.

Cristian Boscheri has performed more than a hundred times on stand-up, alternative comedy and storytelling stages in the Netherlands, the UK and Italy, in English, Italian and Dutch. His comedy plays with language, cognition and paradox, often with his body as much as his words.

In 2025 he was a finalist in both the Comedy Talent Award at TivoliVredenburg and the English Comedy Talent Award at De Kargadoor.
