Pleaser

A solo about people-pleasing, told through breaking, clown, mime and audience interaction.

What do we lose when we keep trying to be liked? What do we gain when we stop?

Pleaser is a solo physical performance by Cristian Boscheri about how people-pleasing is born, how it takes hold of the body, and how hard it is to loosen its grip. Through breakdance, clowning and an intimate stage setup, Cristian traces the desire to be good, helpful and approved of as it reshapes behaviour.

Funny, uncomfortable and sharply observed, Pleaser asks what happens when care turns into a strategy and approval into a negotiation, and what might shift when the disease to please loosens its grip.

“Meer dan een danssolo, meer dan een clownsact.”

“More than a dance solo, more than a clown act.”
